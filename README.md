## 👩‍💻 About me

I'm a 21-year-old Computer Science student who loves turning ideas into polished, user-friendly web experiences. I specialize in **React.js** and the **MERN stack**, and I care deeply about writing clean, maintainable code and building interfaces that people genuinely enjoy using.

Whether it's a complex state management challenge, a pixel-perfect UI, or a full-stack feature — I'm all in. I'm constantly sharpening my skills, exploring new tools, and looking for projects that push me to grow.
